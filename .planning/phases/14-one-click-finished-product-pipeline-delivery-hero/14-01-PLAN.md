# Phase 14 Plan: One-Click Finished Product Pipeline & Delivery Hero

## Objective
Implement the single-click full execution pipeline and finished product delivery hero view that executes storyboard creation, upfront voiceover synthesis, headless Remotion MP4 render, and frame-0 poster baking, immediately presenting a finished, playable, and downloadable video to the user.

## Requirements Addressed
- **ONECLICK-01**: Single-click full pipeline to finished product delivery: runs storyboard + voiceover + local MP4 render + frame-0 poster bake, presenting a finished delivery hero view with playable MP4, downloads, and social captions.

## Vertical Slice Tasks

### Task 1: One-Click Execution Orchestrator & Multi-Stage Stepper
- Implement `useOneClickPipeline` / orchestrator in `src/hooks/useOneClickPipeline.ts`:
  - Chains: Storyboard + Voiceover synthesis + local Remotion render (`POST /api/render`) + status polling until completion.
  - Multi-stage visual progress modal with active states:
    1. ✍️ Formulating Storyboard & Script
    2. 🎙️ Synthesizing Kokoro-82M Voiceover & Ducking
    3. 🎬 Headless Remotion MP4 Rendering
    4. 🖼️ Frame-0 Poster Extraction & FFmpeg Baking
    5. 🚀 Launch Video Ready!

### Task 2: Build `FinishedVideoHero.tsx`
- Delivery hero component displayed immediately upon render completion:
  - Responsive video player auto-playing the rendered MP4 with audio toggle.
  - Prominent primary action: **Download Finished MP4** (`video/mp4`).
  - Settled poster card with **Download Poster** (`image/jpeg`).
  - One-click copy tabs for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Discord marketing copy.
  - "Tweak & Re-render in Studio" expandable toggle allowing power users to view the live Remotion preview and Storyboard Inspector.

### Task 3: End-to-End Verification
- Run complete one-click generation test.
- Verify production build: `npm run build`.
- Verify full test suite: `npm test`.

## Success Criteria
- [ ] User clicks single "Generate Finished Launch Video" button and receives rendered MP4 without manual intermediate steps.
- [ ] Video renders with voiceover narration and ducked upbeat Ende.app music track baked in.
- [ ] Finished Product Hero view displays video player, MP4 download, poster download, and social share copy.
- [ ] All tests passing cleanly.
